## Users and purpose

Anime and manga fans discover titles, browse the airing schedule, and track their progress, lists, and favorites. Episode playback extends the existing anime detail page.

## Design principles

Preserve the existing dark and light themes, yellow primary actions, typography, and shared controls. Keep playback responsive and keyboard accessible. Show loading and unavailable states clearly. Load third-party players only after a viewer chooses to watch.
